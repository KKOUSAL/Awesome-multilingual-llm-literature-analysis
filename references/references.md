## Survey / Review Papers

### 1. A Survey of Multilingual Large Language Models

- **Description:**  
  This paper provides a comprehensive review and unified taxonomy of multilingual large language models, covering training methods, cross-lingual alignment, evaluation, and key challenges across languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://www.cell.com/patterns/fulltext/S2666-3899%2824%2900290-3)

---

### 2. A Survey on Multilingual Large Language Models: Corpora, Alignment, and Bias

- **Description:**  
  This survey examines multilingual LLM training corpora, alignment techniques, and language-specific biases that influence model performance and reliability.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00929)

---

### 3. Multilingual Large Language Models: A Systematic Survey

- **Description:**  
  This systematic survey organizes research on multilingual LLMs across data, architectures, training, evaluation, and applications.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.11072v1)

---

### 4. Multilingual Large Language Model: A Survey of Resources, Taxonomy and Frontiers

- **Description:**  
  This paper reviews multilingual LLM resources and provides a taxonomy of existing approaches while identifying important future research directions.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.04925)

---

### 5. Large Language Models Hallucination: A Comprehensive Survey

- **Description:**  
  This comprehensive survey reviews hallucination in large language models, including detection and mitigation techniques with particular attention to multilingual settings.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06265)

---

### 6. Hallucination to Truth: A Review of Fact-Checking and Factuality Evaluation in Large Language Models

- **Description:**  
  This review examines methods for fact-checking and evaluating factuality in LLMs, including approaches based on retrieval-augmented generation and automated evaluation.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-025-11454-w)

## Foundational Papers

### 7. XTREME: A Massively Multilingual Multi-task Benchmark for Evaluating Cross-lingual Generalization

- **Description:**  
  XTREME introduces a multilingual benchmark covering 40 languages and multiple NLP tasks to evaluate cross-lingual generalization capabilities.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.11080)

---

### 8. XTREME-R: Towards More Challenging and Nuanced Multilingual Evaluation

- **Description:**  
  XTREME-R extends the original XTREME benchmark with more challenging tasks and evaluations for multilingual and cross-lingual language understanding.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07412)

---

### 9. No Language Left Behind: Scaling Human-Centered Machine Translation

- **Description:**  
  This work introduces NLLB-200, a multilingual machine translation system designed to support translation across 200 languages, including many low-resource languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.04672)

---

### 10. SelfCheckGPT: Zero-Resource Black-Box Hallucination Detection for Generative Large Language Models

- **Description:**  
  SelfCheckGPT proposes a black-box method for detecting hallucinations by measuring consistency across multiple responses generated from the same prompt.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896)

---

### 11. The FLORES-101 Evaluation Benchmark for Low-Resource and Multilingual Machine Translation

- **Description:**  
  FLORES-101 provides a multilingual evaluation benchmark for machine translation, with a particular focus on low-resource languages and cross-lingual evaluation.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.30/)

---
## Methods / Algorithms

### 12. MlingConf: A Comprehensive Study of Multilingual Confidence Estimation on Large Language Models

- **Description:**  
  This study investigates confidence estimation in multilingual LLMs and evaluates how reliably models express uncertainty across different languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.12478)

---

### 13. CrossIn: An Efficient Instruction Tuning Approach for Cross-Lingual Knowledge Alignment

- **Description:**  
  CrossIn introduces an instruction-tuning approach for improving cross-lingual knowledge alignment and evaluates consistency across languages using multilingual benchmarks.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.11932)

---

### 14. Improving Cross-Lingual Factual Recall via Consistency-Driven Reinforcement Learning (PolyFact)

- **Description:**  
  PolyFact introduces a large multilingual factual QA dataset and a consistency-driven reinforcement learning approach for improving factual recall across languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06586)

---

### 15. ECLeKTic: A Novel Challenge Set for Evaluation of Cross-Lingual Knowledge Transfer

- **Description:**  
  ECLeKTic evaluates failures in cross-lingual knowledge transfer and provides a challenge set for measuring whether models retain knowledge consistently across languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.21228)

---

### 16. Inference-Time Steering for Cross-Lingual Factual Consistency in LLMs

- **Description:**  
  This work investigates inference-time techniques such as prompting and activation steering to improve factual consistency across different languages.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19243)

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01461)

---

### 18. ArxEval: Evaluating Retrieval and Generation in Language Models for Scientific Literature

- **Description:**  
  ArxEval provides a benchmark for evaluating retrieval and generation capabilities of language models when working with scientific literature.

- **Link:** [Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.10483)

---

### 19. Cross-lingual Extreme Summarization of Scholarly Documents (X-SciTLDR)
