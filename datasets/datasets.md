# Datasets

## 1. FLORES-200/ FLORES+

**Source:** Meta AI

**Description:**  
a benchmark evaluation dataset created by Meta AI for multilingual machine translation, featuring professional human translations of 3,001 sentences across approximately 200 languages.


**Link:**  
https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlanguagedata/flores_plus


## 2. Belebele

**Source:** Meta AI

**Description:**  
Belebele is a large multiple-choice reading comprehension dataset used to test artificial intelligence language models across 122 different languages.

**Link:**  
https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/belebele
